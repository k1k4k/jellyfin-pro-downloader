# Changelog

What's new in each version, as shown in Jellyfin's plugin catalog.

## 0.1.0

- "Download all" on a series, season, album or collection no longer starts every download at once: a window lets you pick seasons, episodes, albums or tracks, with their sizes and the total.
- Also available on artists, playlists and folders.
- Three ways to download: into a folder you choose, with everything organized (Chrome, Edge, Opera over HTTPS), as a queue (any browser, one file after another) or as ZIP files (one per season / album, or a single one), generated on the fly and resumable.
- Subtitles, .nfo files, images and lyrics next to the media are included (can be unchecked).
- A download manager (download icon at the top) shows progress and speed, with pause, resume, retry and cancel. Unfinished downloads are offered again at the next visit.
- A single movie or episode downloads directly, without the window.
- Server-side limits: simultaneous downloads per user and in total, speed per user. Each user's "Allow media downloading" permission is respected.
